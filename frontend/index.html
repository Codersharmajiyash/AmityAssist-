Frequently Asked Questions

How long does withdrawal processing take?

Verification takes 1-2 days, clearances take 3-5 days, and finance refund takes 7-10 working days.

How to obtain a Bonafide Certificate?

Submit the filled Bonafide form at Registrar Window Block A. Processing turnaround is 2 working days.
